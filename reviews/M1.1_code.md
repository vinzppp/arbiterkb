## Findings (most severe first)

- **[MAJOR] `arbiter_kb/rules.py:78-88` (R5) with `arbiter_kb/beliefs.py:123-146` — R5 counts the same constraint twice when covers happen on different frames.**
  - **What is wrong.** On every frame where a cube is covered, R5 recombines all hidden, unmixed cubes. That includes cubes whose distributions are already R5 outputs from an earlier cover. Those marginals are no longer independent, so the "at most one cube per container" constraint between them is applied a second time.
  - **Pure math.** g={a:.6,b:.4}, r={a:.3,b:.7}. One pass of `joint_exclusive` gives g(a)=0.778. A second pass on the outputs gives 0.925.
  - **Through the full system.** I used `ArbiterKBSystem`. Setup: bin_a at (0,0) and bin_b at (0.04,0) cover cube_g at (0,0) and cube_r at (0.015,0) at t=5. Later, bin_c at (0.30,0) covers cube_b at t=10.
    - Result: cube_r goes from bin_b 0.788 to 0.913, and cube_g from 0.80 to 0.914.
    - cube_b has no mass on bin_a or bin_b, so its cover carries no information about g or r. They should not change.
    - 0.913 is above τ_hi = 0.8, so a wrong answer here would become a silent failure.
  - **Why MAJOR and not BLOCK.** The code does what spec §3.5 says, so the flaw is in the spec. No current source produces staggered covers: synthetic covers all happen at t=32, and R5 emitted 0 ops in the table. No reported number is affected. If any source ever produces covers on different frames, this becomes BLOCK.
  - **Reproduce.** Put the 3-cube, 15-frame scenario above into a script, or the two-line `joint_exclusive(joint_exclusive(...))` check.
  - **Suggested fix.** Keep one explicit joint table over the hidden cubes (at most 3 cubes × 5 keys = 125 states) and update it when a cube is covered. Or limit R5 to the cubes covered on this frame and say in §3.5 and NOTES that staggered covers are not handled. Either way, add the scenario above as a test asserting cube_r is unchanged by a far cover.

- **[MINOR] `rules.py:78-80` — R5's "mixed" filter is on/off, while the mixing amount varies.** One MixDist of any size excludes the cube from R5 for the rest of the episode. Suggested fix: exclude a cube only when the total mixed w is above a threshold in `RuleParams`, or keep the joint (fix above).
